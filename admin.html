            Run the auto translator

            Series list

            The runner works this list from the top: the first series until all its episodes are translated (it continues the next day from where it stopped), then the next one. Every Sunday up to 10 new series without a Sinhala subtitle are added at the bottom (what visitors translated / searched first, then new releases, then random good series).

            Ready translations (auto-runner)

            Made by GitHub Actions every day in the last hour before the Gemini reset (12:40 PM Sri Lanka). Shown to everybody on the home page. Titles to translate: auto-queue.json in the repo (by hand + auto-discover of new series without a Sinhala subtitle). Run it now: GitHub, Actions, Auto translate, Run workflow.

            Film pages from the library

            Every film that a signed-in visitor translated gets its own page (/film/…) with the poster, story, trailer and a download. New ones are made automatically. Press the button once to make pages for the films that were translated before. It is safe to press again: pages that exist are skipped.

            Browser notifications

            Visitors who pressed "Turn on" get these even when the site is closed. A message is also sent automatically (at most once every 6 hours) when a NEW title reaches the Ready list. Needs VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY and VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY in Vercel.

            Sinhala story intros

            The short spoiler-free Sinhala story intro on every title page. They are made when a page is opened; this makes them for ALL titles now (2 titles at a time, uses the one summary key). Tick the box to replace the ones that already exist (for example after the spoiler rules changed).
