Frequently Asked Questions

What is SinSubz?

SinSubz is a free-to-use tool that translates English movie and TV subtitles into natural, spoken Sinhala using artificial intelligence.

How do I get Sinhala subtitles for a movie or TV series?

Search for the movie or series, pick an English subtitle and translate it to Sinhala, or upload your own SRT file. When the translation finishes you can download the Sinhala SRT file.

Which subtitle format does SinSubz support?

SinSubz currently supports SRT subtitle files.

Can I sync a Sinhala subtitle to my movie file?

Yes. Use the Sync tool: upload your Sinhala SRT, choose your movie file (only its file name is used, the video is never uploaded) and pick the matching English subtitle. SinSubz then corrects the timing of the Sinhala subtitle and keeps its text unchanged.
