What is SubLanka AI?

SubLanka AI is a subtitle translation tool designed to make English subtitle translation into Sinhala easier and more convenient.

What Can You Do?

You can search for movies or TV series, find available English subtitles, upload your own SRT subtitle file, translate subtitle text, and download the translated subtitle as an SRT file.

Simple Workflow

01

Search

Search for a movie or TV series.

02

Select Subtitle

Choose an available English subtitle.

03

Translate

Translate the subtitle into Sinhala.

04

Download

Download the translated SRT file.

Built for Subtitle Lovers

The goal of SubLanka AI is simple: reduce the effort required to turn English subtitles into readable Sinhala subtitles while keeping the process easy to understand.
